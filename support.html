Restore Dugout Pro

In the app, open Settings → Plans & restore purchases → Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Keep your team data

Dugout stores team and game data on your device. Export a JSON backup before deleting the app or changing devices. A lineup PDF is for sharing a single lineup, not for restoring your teams.
