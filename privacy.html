Privacy policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Team data

Dugout stores team names, player names, eligibility, attendance, lineups, and game history locally on your device. There is no Dugout account or remote roster database.

Exports and deletion

You can export a JSON backup or a lineup PDF through your device's share sheet. These files can contain player names, and you choose where they go. You can remove local data by deleting teams in the app or uninstalling it. Uninstalling does not delete copies that you exported elsewhere.

Purchases

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase information needed to provide and restore Dugout Pro. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user ID and purchase history to validate subscriptions, unlock Pro, and provide subscription analytics. Dugout does not send player names or lineup data with purchase requests.

Advertising and analytics

Dugout does not use advertising or general app-usage analytics. RevenueCat provides purchase analytics only.

Privacy requests

Email marouansnaoui@icloud.com or visit our support page. Please do not include player names or private team details in your request.
